ML Model Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Linear Regression Trend Model

A simple linear trend model $y = a + bx$ was selected because the available national historical series contains five annual points (2020–2024). It fits a linear trend rate of +13,723 cases per year with a high fit score of R² = 0.949.
